  WBA.csv, 3333.HK.csv, SHF.JO.csv

Download the "historical data" CSV from Investing.com, Nasdaq.com, MarketWatch or similar.
The file needs a date column (Date) and a price column (Close, Close/Last, Price or Adj Close).
Prices must be in whole units of the share's currency: rand (not cents) for JSE shares,
HK dollars for Hong Kong, US dollars for US shares.

These prices are used instead of Yahoo's on the same dates and fill in dates Yahoo doesn't have.
