Companies

Returns run from the latest call to the latest weekly close: total return for calls 3 to 12 months old, annualised for older calls, and calls under 3 months old are marked too early. Trend: up above +5%, down below −5%, otherwise flat; flat counts as no growth. Versus the S&P 500: the share's return minus the index's, on the same basis; a bullish call is right above +5%, a bearish call below −5%, and anything in between is inconclusive and not scored. Both verdicts use Piet's latest call on each company and measure the share and the S&P 500 from the date of that call to the latest weekly close. The chart starts at his first call; the S&P 500 (converted to rand) and the Cockroach Fund are rebased to the share price on that date so all lines start together. Prices are Friday closes, converted to rand at that week's exchange rate.
